Mathematical Formulation: f(n) = g(n) + h(n)

g(n) is the exact cost from start to node n. h(n) is the estimated heuristic to goal. When \(h(n) \le h^*(n)\) (admissible), A* is guaranteed to return the optimal shortest path.

Euclidean vs. Manhattan Metric

Euclidean \( \sqrt{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2} \) reflects continuous straight-line distance, while Manhattan \( |\Delta x| + |\Delta y| \) represents orthogonal grid corridor travel.

Dijkstra vs. A*

Dijkstra expands in concentric circles where \(h(n) = 0\), expanding significantly more nodes. A* biases its frontier towards the destination goal.
